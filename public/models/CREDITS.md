# Third-party models

## Cessna 172SP — `free_cessna_172sp.glb`

"FREE Cessna 172SP" (https://skfb.ly/pwATP) by NLM is licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Used with the modifications described in `src/c172-glb.js`: ground equipment
removed, cabin door closed, glazing retuned, lift struts added, and the flaps,
ailerons and elevator split and hinged so they can be driven by the simulator.

## Airbus A380-800 — `airbus_a380full_interior_hd.glb`

"Airbus A380(full interior hd)"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/airbus-a380full-interior-hd-3155062bc0e545f897e8d766dbe7299e)
by Raakesh.Madan is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Used with the modifications described in `src/a380-glb.js`: the cabin fit-out
removed and the flight deck kept, glazing retuned, and the undercarriage carved
out of the meshes it was merged into and hung on its trunnions so the simulator
can raise and lower it.

## Lockheed Martin F-35A — `f-35a_lightning_ii.glb`

"F-35A Lightning II"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/f-35a-lightning-ii-a06d6113cfb44a0aa7b8f17106aca9c4)
by shangus930 is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Used with the modifications described in `src/f35-glb.js`: scaled from the real
aircraft's span and adapted to the simulator's control, undercarriage, lighting,
and cockpit-camera systems.

## Daher TBM 930 — `daher_tbm_930.glb`

"Daher TBM 930"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/daher-tbm-930-ba21567b779040038081f084fc528a44)
by helijah (Emmanuel Baranger) is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Converted from the author's original OBJ by `scripts/build-tbm-model.mjs`, and
used with the modifications described in `src/tbm930-glb.js`: the ventral fins
removed, the control surfaces hinged on their measured hinge lines, the
undercarriage and its doors hung on trunnions so the simulator can raise and
lower it, and the glazing retuned.
